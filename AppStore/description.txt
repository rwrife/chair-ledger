Chair Ledger is a planned offline appointment book for solo stylists and barbers. Set your services, cleanup buffers, and working hours; record appointments; spot scheduling conflicts; and carry a clear day sheet on phone, tablet, or Windows. Designed for private, organizer-operated schedules without client accounts, online booking, or payment processing. Export your own data when you choose. Features described here are planned; this repository currently contains documentation and artwork, not a released app.
