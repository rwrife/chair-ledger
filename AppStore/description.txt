Chair Ledger is an offline appointment book for solo stylists and barbers. Set your services, cleanup buffers, and working hours; record appointments; spot scheduling conflicts; and carry a clear day sheet on phone, tablet, or Windows. Designed for private, organizer-operated schedules without client accounts, online booking, or payment processing. Export your own data when you choose. App available as source; no store release yet. Local device data is not encrypted by the app. JSON backups include private notes and client labels; CSV day sheets redact labels by default. Protect exported files.
